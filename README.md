#### Cyber Resilience Based on Intelligent Agents and Model Context Protocol (MCP): An AI Safety & Security Framework 

Abstract — The adoption of intelligent agents powered by foundational artificial intelligence models has generated new challenges in security, privacy, governance, and digital resilience. An open protocol to facilitate interoperability between AI models, tools, data sources, and external services enables the construction of more dynamic and collaborative agent ecosystems. However, this connectivity expands the attack surface and introduces risks associated with context manipulation, unauthorized access to resources, and the execution of unauthorized actions. This work proposes a cyber resilience framework based on intelligent agents, integrating security principles into the development and use of artificial intelligence, system protection, and continuous verification of users, devices, and resources before granting access. The proposal incorporates governance mechanisms, contextual validation, continuous monitoring, and traceability of automated decisions, contributing to the development of systems that use artificial intelligence to reduce operational risks with resilience techniques.
